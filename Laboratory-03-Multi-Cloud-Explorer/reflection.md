# Mission Reflection

## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud because of its services for artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. I learned that cloud providers offer specialized tools that can help organizations develop and manage modern applications. However, I also understand that AWS and Azure have their own strengths and may be more suitable for different business situations.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud provide computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and identity management services. They allow organizations to deploy applications without having to manage all physical infrastructure themselves. Although the names of their services differ, many of their services perform similar functions. All three platforms also provide tools for scaling and monitoring workloads.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements help determine which cloud provider is most suitable for an organization. A startup may prioritize cost and scalability, while a university using Microsoft products may benefit from Azure integration. An AI research company may prioritize machine learning tools and computing performance, whereas an international online store may focus on global availability and automatic scaling. Therefore, cloud selection should consider budget, security, workload, existing technology, and technical expertise.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

I learned that a Cloud Solutions Consultant must understand both technology and business needs. Comparing the three platforms helped me recognize that there is no single provider that is best for every situation. I also practiced researching official documentation, matching equivalent services, analyzing client scenarios, and explaining recommendations. These skills are useful when planning cloud solutions for real organizations.
